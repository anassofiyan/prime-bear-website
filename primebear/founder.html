Meet Our Founder

Aarav Malhotra

Founder & Lead Consultant, Prime Bear

Aarav spent nine years running production kitchens and opening bakery outlets before starting Prime Bear — work that taught him exactly where most first-time founders get stuck: underpricing menus, undersized kitchens, and staff who were never properly trained on the recipes they're expected to execute.

Prime Bear exists to close that gap — pairing hands-on kitchen experience with the planning discipline of a consultancy, so founders don't have to learn these lessons the expensive way.

“We believe every great bakery starts with a strong foundation.”
